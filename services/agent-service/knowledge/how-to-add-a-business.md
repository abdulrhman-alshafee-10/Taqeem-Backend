# How to add a business on Taqeem

1. Sign in (or create an account).
2. Go to **Add a business** in the header menu.
3. Fill in: name, address (with map pin), categories, price tier, hours, phone, website.
4. Submit. The business appears immediately as **unclaimed**.
5. To claim ownership, use **Claim this business** and upload proof (utility bill, tax record).
6. After approval, the business shows up under **My businesses** and you can reply to reviews.
